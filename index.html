What is the DeepSeek anime maid meme?

DeepSeek-chan, also called Whale-chan or the DeepSeek whale girl, is a fan-made anime personification associated with DeepSeek’s whale identity. The blue-haired maid appears in community art, reaction stickers, and desktop pets. Chinese fan names include DeepSeek 娘、鲸鱼娘和大肥鱼; a recurring joke gives her an appetite for rice and a talent for avoiding work.

Banke turns that playful idea into a focus companion: this time, the whale maid is the one reminding you to finish. These are independently made fan-inspired characters, with no affiliation with or endorsement by DeepSeek, Anthropic, or OpenAI.

A focus ritual for coding, studying, and side projects

  1. Choose one task and one companion. Swipe the character or use the side arrows to switch between DeepSeek, Claude, and ChatGPT.
  2. Set your session. Open 计时设置 (the gear button). Start small with 3 + 2 minutes, choose 25 + 5, or customize both durations.
  3. Start, then wrap up. Press 开始专注. After focus time, the second countdown gives you time to finish. Pause when you need to; slide right to reset.

Try one bug fix, one eval review, or one section of a paper per session. For a vibe-coding session, use the wrap-up time to read the diff and decide what to test next.

About the timer

Can I use this as an AI avatar Pomodoro timer?

You can use Banke to time a 25-minute focus session with an anime avatar. Its 25 + 5 preset means 25 minutes of focus followed by 5 minutes of wrap-up time, with overtime reminders after that. It does not automatically alternate work and rest or count Pomodoro rounds. Pause or reset when you are ready for a break.

Does the avatar use a live AI model or chat with me?

No. “AI companion” describes the AI-themed characters. Expressions and bundled Mandarin voice clips react to timer events and taps. Banke does not send prompts to DeepSeek, Claude, or ChatGPT, and it needs no model subscription or API key.

Is this a DeepSeek desktop pet or a Claude Code extension?

Banke is a browser focus companion. Keep it open beside your editor or study material on desktop, or use it in your phone browser. It does not install an always-on-top desktop pet or connect to your coding agent.

Is it free, and what gets saved?

The timer is free and requires no account. Your browser saves your selected companion, durations, sound setting, and overtime preferences locally. Refreshing or reopening starts a fresh, stopped session. The site uses Vercel Analytics for usage measurement.

Are the interface and voices available in English?

The timer controls and character voices are currently in Simplified Chinese and Mandarin. This English guide explains the controls. You can mute speech with the speaker button and use the visual countdown.
