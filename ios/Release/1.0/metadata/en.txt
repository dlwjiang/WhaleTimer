A cozy corner for your next focus session.

Tick & Kin pairs a Pomodoro timer with illustrated companions, gentle voice cues and a tactile flip clock. Study, read or work with a little company beside you.

START WITH THREE FREE COMPANIONS
Mochi, Pip and Pebble are included. Tap your companion for a little reaction, switch between them, and settle into their painted rooms.

FIND YOUR RHYTHM
Set your focus, short-break and long-break lengths. Turn either kind of break off if you prefer. Pause, resume or start over when you need to. Optional reminders let you know when a session ends.

A COMPANION FOR YOUR DESK
Use portrait or landscape on iPhone and iPad. On iPhone Duo, the layout adapts as you fold: a companion above and a timer below in tabletop mode, with room artwork arranged around the hinge. Changing the layout keeps your timer going.

LITTLE VOICES, AT YOUR PACE
Hear character voice cues or mute them and keep the floating captions. Interface and language support are always free: English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French and German.

CHOOSE MORE COMPANY
Three optional, one-time purchases each add three companions and their scenes:
• Midnight Café: Jun, Bean and Maple
• Moonlight Academy: Luna, Atlas and Wisp
• Orbital Night Shift: Nova, Orbit and Comet

The three-pack collection includes all nine paid companions from these launch packs. Future packs are not included. Paid companions require an in-app purchase; the timer and three starter companions remain free.

No account. No ads. No subscription. The timer and bundled voices work offline. Purchases and restoration require access to the App Store.

A focus timer with personality—not an AI chat service or an app blocker.
