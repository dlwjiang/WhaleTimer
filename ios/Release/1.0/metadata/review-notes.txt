Tick & Kin is a Pomodoro timer with illustrated companions. No account, login, server connection, or demo credentials are required to use the free timer.

Mochi, Pip and Pebble are included free. All nine interface languages and language support are free. Paid content is cosmetic companion artwork, voices and scenes, sold through four non-consumable StoreKit products. There are no subscriptions, external digital purchases, ads, user-generated content or AI chat.

To test the timer quickly: Settings (gear) → Quick try · 1 min focus + 1 min break → Done → Start focusing. A short lead-in precedes a fresh focus session. Breaks start explicitly rather than automatically. Both short and long breaks may be disabled separately. The timer restores its deadline after backgrounding.

To review purchases: Settings → Companion packs. Select Midnight Café, Moonlight Academy or Orbital Night Shift. Each pack includes three companions. Scroll down the pack detail to its purchase button. The three-pack collection appears at the bottom of the main packs page for users who own no paid pack; it includes only the three named launch packs. It is hidden after an individual pack purchase to avoid selling overlapping content. Restore purchases is on the packs page.

All twelve companions and their voice recordings are bundled; no content download is required after purchase. The store needs a connection to load prices, purchase or restore. Previously verified ownership is read from StoreKit. A refund removes the affected entitlement, preserving independent purchases.

On iPhone Duo, the layout responds to folding and orientation. Tabletop mode places the companion above the timer with a painted ledge aligned to the hinge. Portrait/landscape and iPad layouts share the same companion interactions. Folding does not start or reset a session.

Notifications are optional local session reminders. The app does not record speech, request camera/microphone/location access, or include app analytics. Privacy, support and terms links are in Settings → Help and legal.
